About Our Institution

A Heritage of Rigor, Innovation & African Pride

Located in Lekki, Lagos, Bliss Academy combines the highest standards of international education with rich Nigerian cultural heritage and character training. We foster critical inquiry, scientific curiosity, and moral groundedness across Early Years, Primary, and College levels.

Our Mission

To provide a transformative, world-class education that empowers Nigerian youth to achieve academic mastery, innovate with technology, and lead ethically.

Our Vision

To stand as West Africa's leading center of scholastic excellence, recognized globally for groundbreaking STEM innovation, artistic expression, and leadership.
