About Our School

Building Strong Minds & Godly Character in Agbor

Situated in the serene environment of Alihame, Agbor, Delta State, Bliss Academy offers comprehensive education from Early Years through Senior Secondary. We are dedicated to providing a balanced academic foundation that nurtures intellectual growth, technological literacy, and moral uprightness.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-standard primary and secondary education that instills character, critical thinking, and academic excellence in every child.

Our Vision

To be the foremost basic and secondary academic institution in Delta State, recognized for nurturing well-rounded leaders of high integrity.
